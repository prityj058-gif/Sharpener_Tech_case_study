# Business Analytics Case Studies | Data Analytics Portfolio

## About This Repository

Welcome to my Business Analytics Portfolio!

This repository contains five business analytics case studies focused on solving real-world business problems using data analysis, visualization, and data-driven recommendations.

Each project explores a different business area, including retail sales, customer retention, product profitability, marketing performance, and inventory optimization.

The objective of these projects is to transform raw data into meaningful insights that can support better business decisions.

## Projects Included

### 1. FashionWave — Retail Sales Analysis

**Business Area:** Retail Sales Analytics

**Objective:** Analyze sales performance, profitability, discount impact, customer behavior, and seasonal trends.

**Key Areas of Analysis:**
- Sales and profit performance
- Product category analysis
- Discount impact on profit margins
- Monthly and seasonal sales trends
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Store performance

**Business Recommendations:**
- Optimize discount strategies to protect profit margins.
- Use seasonal trends to plan promotions.
- Improve customer retention through loyalty initiatives.
- Investigate underperforming stores and product categories.

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets

### 2. Boxify — Customer Churn Analysis

**Business Area:** Customer Analytics

**Objective:** Understand customer churn patterns, identify customer segments with higher churn, and recommend strategies to improve retention.

**Key Areas of Analysis:**
- Overall customer churn rate
- Churn by age group and subscription plan
- Customer engagement and satisfaction
- Early customer churn patterns
- Revenue associated with churned customers
- Rule-based customer risk screening

**Business Recommendations:**
- Improve onboarding and engagement during the first six months.
- Introduce targeted retention campaigns.
- Monitor churn across customer segments.
- Track retention KPIs and evaluate the effectiveness of interventions.

**Tools:** Excel, MySQL, Power BI

### 3. TastyBites — Product Pricing and Profitability Analysis

**Business Area:** Pricing and Profitability Analytics

**Objective:** Evaluate product-level revenue and profitability, understand the impact of discounts, and identify opportunities to improve pricing decisions.

**Key Areas of Analysis:**
- Revenue and profit performance
- Product-level profitability
- Profit margin comparison
- Discount impact analysis
- Identification of high- and low-margin products
- Pricing and promotional opportunities

**Business Recommendations:**
- Use targeted discounts instead of unnecessary deep discounts.
- Develop bundles based on product profitability.
- Monitor contribution margins before introducing promotions.
- Review pricing strategies while considering customer demand.

**Tools:** Excel, Google Sheets

### 4. GlowUp — Marketing Campaign ROI Analysis

**Business Area:** Marketing Analytics

**Objective:** Compare marketing channel and campaign performance to identify opportunities for improving marketing efficiency and allocating the next-quarter budget.

**Key Areas of Analysis:**
- Marketing spend and attributed revenue
- Return on investment (ROI)
- Cost per acquisition or conversion (CPA)
- Leads and conversions by channel
- Campaign-level performance
- Monthly marketing trends

**Business Recommendations:**
- Prioritize efficient channels while considering conversion volume.
- Focus on improving Social Media lead quality and conversion efficiency.
- Review campaign performance regularly.
- Test budget changes and measure their impact before making larger reallocations.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Excel, Power BI

### 5. ToyJoy — Inventory Optimization

**Business Area:** Inventory and Supply Chain Analytics

**Objective:** Analyze inventory performance, identify excess inventory and stockout issues, and recommend strategies for improving inventory planning.

**Key Areas of Analysis:**
- Total units sold and revenue
- Inventory turnover
- Excess inventory value
- Stockout rate and availability
- Monthly and seasonal demand
- Product, location, and sales channel performance

**Business Recommendations:**
- Use seasonal demand forecasting.
- Establish product-level reorder points.
- Maintain safety stock based on demand variability.
- Rebalance excess stock across locations.
- Monitor inventory KPIs through a Power BI dashboard.

**Tools:** Excel, Power BI

## Business Problem-Solving Approach

My approach to each case study follows these steps:

1. **Understand the business problem** — Define the objective and key business questions.
2. **Prepare the data** — Review data quality, handle relevant issues, and standardize information.
3. **Analyze the data** — Calculate KPIs, compare segments, and identify trends.
4. **Generate insights** — Explain what the findings mean for the business.
5. **Recommend actions** — Suggest practical improvements supported by the analysis.
6. **Measure performance** — Identify KPIs that can be monitored to evaluate future results.
